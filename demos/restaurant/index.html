Our story

Simple food, honest fire.

We opened Ember & Oak with one idea: cook fewer things, but cook them properly. Every dish touches the wood oven, and every ingredient comes from producers we know by name.

No tasting menus, no fuss. Just a warm room, good bread and a table that is yours for the evening.
